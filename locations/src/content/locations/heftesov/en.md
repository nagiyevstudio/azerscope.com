## About the Location

The Haftasov (Həftəsov) observation location is set in the mountainous terrain of the Ismayilli District of Azerbaijan, along the southern slopes of the Greater Caucasus. The campsite is positioned on high mountain plateaus at over 1,500–1,800 m elevation, completely secluded from urban light pollution and low-altitude atmospheric humidity.

The surrounding mountain ridges create a natural shield against stray light, delivering dark, high-contrast skies.

---

## Astronomical Conditions

- **Bortle Scale:** Class 3.7 (clean rural/mountain sky, SQM ~21.75 mag/arcsec²).
- **Milky Way:** Brilliantly visible spanning across the entire sky with intricate dust lanes and galactic core structure.
- **Horizon:** Broad mountain panorama. Southern and western skies are virtually pristine.
- **Astrophotography:** Superb conditions for wide-field nightscapes, deep-sky nebulae (Cygnus and Sagittarius regions), and star cluster imaging.

---

## Travel & Logistics

- **Distance from Baku:** ~150–170 km (via Shamakhi — Ismayilli corridor followed by an unpaved mountain ascent).
- **Vehicle:** A high-clearance vehicle or 4WD SUV is recommended for mountain tracks.
- **Infrastructure:** Wild camping site. Bring complete off-grid gear: tents, warm sleeping bags, fresh water, and portable power stations.

---

## Stargazing Tips

1. **Mountain Temperature:** Nights can be chilly even in summer; pack thermal layers, windbreakers, and warm headwear.
2. **Dew Control:** Mountain humidity may cause lens condensation after midnight — use dew heaters for telescopes and camera lenses.
3. **Power Reserves:** Cold weather lowers battery performance; ensure plenty of portable power bank capacity.
