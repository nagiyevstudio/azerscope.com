## About the Location

This observation location is nestled within the scenic rolling foothills of the Khizi District of Azerbaijan, roughly 85–90 km north of Baku. Situated away from direct coastal highway glare and urban illumination, the spot provides a convenient and dark escape for stargazers.

The natural undulating terrain acts as a buffer against ground-level light trespass, opening panoramic vistas of the night sky. The location serves as a premier venue for annual astrocamps during the peak of the Perseid meteor shower.

---

## Astronomical Conditions

- **Bortle Scale:** Class 4.0 (rural/suburban transition sky, SQM ~21.65 mag/arcsec²).
- **Milky Way:** Prominently visible above the horizon, arching gracefully across the night sky.
- **Meteor Showers:** Exceptional conditions for counting and photographing the Perseids (peaking August 12–13), Geminids, and other major meteor showers.
- **Horizon:** 360° open horizon with picturesque hilly foregrounds for nightscape astrophotography.

---

## Travel & Logistics

- **Distance from Baku:** ~85–90 km via Baku — Guba highway and the Gilazi — Khizi road.
- **Vehicle:** Fully accessible by standard 2WD passenger cars (sedans / hatchbacks / crossovers). The dirt track off the paved road is short and gentle in dry conditions.
- **Infrastructure:** Off-grid field site. Bring camping chairs, picnic mats, drinking water, and warm drinks. Mobile network coverage is reliable.

---

## Stargazing Tips

1. **Meteor Watching Setup:** Reclining camp chairs or foam sleeping mats are best for comfortably viewing the zenith for hours.
2. **Photography:** Use a fast wide-angle lens (f/1.4–f/2.8) on a tripod with interval shooting to capture fireball meteor streaks over the Milky Way.
3. **Clothing:** Foothill breezes can bring crisp temperatures at night — pack a warm windbreaker or hoodie.
