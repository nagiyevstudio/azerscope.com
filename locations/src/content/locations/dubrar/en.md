## About the Location

Mount Dubrar (Dübrar dağı) is situated in the Khizi District of Azerbaijan, along the southeastern spur of the Greater Caucasus Range. The observation campsite sits at an elevation of approximately **2,200 meters** above sea level, making it one of the premier high-altitude spots for astronomical expeditions and astrocamping.

Due to its significant elevation, the site rises above ground-level atmospheric haze and moisture, providing exceptional sky transparency and stable seeing conditions.

---

## Astronomical Conditions

- **Bortle Scale:** Class 3–4 (rural/mountain transition sky, SQM ~21.76 mag/arcsec²).
- **Milky Way:** Prominently visible from horizon to horizon, showing rich dust lanes and structure across Cygnus, Sagittarius, and Scutum.
- **Horizon:** 360° unobstructed panoramic view. Southern and western horizons remain clean and free from major light domes.
- **Astrophotography:** Superb conditions for deep-sky imaging — globular clusters (M13 in Hercules), galaxies (Andromeda M31, Triangulum M33), and emission nebulae.

---

## Travel & Logistics

- **Distance from Baku:** ~110–130 km (via Khizi / Altiaghaj route, followed by an unpaved mountain track).
- **Vehicle:** A 4WD / high-clearance SUV is strongly recommended, especially for the steep final mountain ascent.
- **Infrastructure:** Completely wild, off-grid location. There is no running water, grid electricity, or commercial facilities at the summit. Bring all necessary drinking water, food, fuel, and battery power.

---

## Astrocamping Tips

1. **Warm Clothing:** Even during mid-summer, night temperatures at 2,200 m can drop to +8...+12 °C (and near freezing in spring/autumn). Pack thermal layers, windproof jackets, and warm hats.
2. **Wind Preparedness:** Mountain ridges can experience gusty winds. Secure tents with heavy-duty guy lines and weigh down telescope tripods.
3. **Power & Dew Prevention:** Bring portable power stations (LiFePO4) and dew heaters to prevent lens fogging during humid night shifts.
4. **Red Light Only:** Keep white light to a minimum and use dim red headlamps to preserve dark-adapted night vision across the camp.
